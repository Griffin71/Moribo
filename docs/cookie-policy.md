## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies
These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They include security-related cookies and session cookies.
- **Examples:** Session identifiers, security tokens
- **Opt-out:** You cannot opt-out of these cookies

### Performance & Analytics Cookies
These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website.
- **Examples:** Google Analytics, page view tracking
- **Opt-out:** Available through cookie settings

### Functional Cookies
These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make and provide enhanced functionality.
- **Examples:** Language preferences, user preferences
- **Opt-out:** Available through cookie settings

### Marketing & Advertising Cookies
These cookies track your online activity and display personalized advertisements.
- **Examples:** Retargeting pixels, conversion tracking
- **Opt-out:** Available through cookie settings

## 3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:
- Ensure the website functions properly
- Improve user experience and website performance
- Analyze user behavior and usage patterns
- Personalize content and recommendations
- Remember your preferences and login information
- Deliver targeted advertising
- Prevent fraudulent activity
- Comply with legal requirements

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

We may allow third-party service providers to place cookies on your device:

## 5. How to Control Cookies

### Browser Settings
Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent.

### Cookie Controls on This Site
Use the toggle switches on our Cookie Settings page to enable or disable different types of cookies.

### Opt-Out Options
- Network Advertising Initiative: https://www.networkadvertising.org/managing/opt_out.asp
- Digital Advertising Alliance: https://www.aboutads.info/choices

## 6. Retention of Cookie Data

- **Session cookies:** Deleted when you close your browser
- **Persistent cookies:** May be retained for up to 2 years
- **Analytics data:** Typically retained for 26 months
