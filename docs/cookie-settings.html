1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience. Cookies may be persistent (stored for a longer period) or session-based (cleared when you close your browser).

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They include security-related cookies and session cookies. You cannot opt-out of these cookies as they are required for the website to operate.

Examples: Session identifiers, security tokens

Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. They collect information about which pages are visited, how long visitors stay, and any errors encountered.

Examples: Google Analytics, page view tracking

Enable Analytics Cookies

Functional Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make and provide enhanced functionality. They may remember your language preference, login information, or customized features.

Examples: Language preferences, user preferences

Enable Functional Cookies

Marketing & Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to track your online activity and to display personalized advertisements based on your interests. They may be set by us or by third-party advertising partners.

Examples: Retargeting pixels, conversion tracking

Enable Marketing Cookies

3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the website functions properly
  • Improve user experience and website performance
  • Analyze user behavior and usage patterns
  • Personalize content and recommendations
  • Remember your preferences and login information
  • Deliver targeted advertising
  • Prevent fraudulent activity
  • Comply with legal requirements

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may allow third-party service providers to place cookies on your device for analytics, advertising, and functional purposes. These include:

  • Google Analytics
  • Social media platforms
  • Advertising partners
  • Analytics and performance tools

5. How to Control Cookies

5.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. Please note that blocking cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

5.2 Cookie Controls on This Site

Use the toggle switches above to enable or disable different types of cookies. Your preferences will be saved in your browser.

5.3 Opt-Out Options

You can opt-out of certain advertising networks by visiting:

  • Network Advertising Initiative
  • Digital Advertising Alliance

6. Retention of Cookie Data

Different cookies are retained for different periods:

  • Session cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies: May be retained for up to 2 years
  • Analytics data: Typically retained for 26 months
